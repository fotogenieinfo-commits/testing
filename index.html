An unhurried studio pause between train arrivals.

FOTOGENIE replaces harsh station fluorescent lighting and cluttered arcade booths with a brushed steel architectural enclosure. Step inside off the concourse, tap your transit card, and leave in under twenty seconds with a tactile four-frame silver-halide-style print.
